# The water cycle — STE-inspired explanation

**STE-inspired simplified English — approximate, not validated for ASD-STE100 conformity**

## The explanation

The water cycle moves water between oceans, air, and land. Vapor is water in gas form.
The Sun heats oceans and lakes. Heated water changes into vapor, and the vapor rises.
Higher up, the air is colder. In the cold air, the vapor changes into tiny drops. These
drops form clouds. The drops join together and become heavy. Heavy drops then fall as
rain or snow. Some of this water soaks into the ground. Some water flows back to the
ocean in rivers. After these steps, the cycle repeats. The cycle does not have a
starting point. The cycle is never perfectly closed, because water can stay stored in
ice and underground for long periods.
