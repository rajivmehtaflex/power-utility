## The explanation

The cathedral clock was first documented on 14 March 1554. On that date, the city
council paid a watchmaker for repairs. The clock does not strike during the night
hours. The clock has never been connected to the town's bell network. The astronomical
dial was repainted in 1702. The painter is not known for certain. The painting style

dial likely cost more than the movement itself, because guild records list a single
large payment that year. Restoration work in 1974 replaced the escapement. The original
parts are probably still in the city museum. The clock is not the oldest in the region.
